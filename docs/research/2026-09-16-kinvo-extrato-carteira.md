# Research: Kinvo `extrato-carteira` XLSX exports

- **Ticket:** [luiznaac/portfolio-2#118](https://github.com/luiznaac/portfolio-2/issues/118) — "Research: Kinvo extrato-carteira exports"
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Branch:** `research/kinvo-extrato-carteira` (worktree `../.worktrees/portfolio-2-research-kinvo-extrato`)
- **Samples:** `references/kinvo-extrato/kinvo--extrato-carteira 15_09_2026 3_25.xlsx` and
  `... 3_26.xlsx` (git-ignored; read from the main checkout). Parsed with `openpyxl 3.1.5` and by
  reading the raw OOXML parts from the zip.
- **Question (verbatim from the issue):**

> What are the files in `references/kinvo-extrato/` (`kinvo--extrato-carteira 15_09_2026 3_25.xlsx`,
> `kinvo--extrato-carteira 15_09_2026 3_26.xlsx`)? Which Kinvo screen/report produced them, and what
> fields do they carry — positions (quantity, average price), asset classes, proventos?
>
> Do they change the closed conclusion of "Research: Kinvo export for initial positions" (no positions
> export; only a Premium, web-only IR auxiliary XLSX)? If they do carry positions, does "Kinvo as an
> ongoing source of balances" deserve a re-scope on the map?

## Answer (short)

- The two files are **two different filtered exports of the Kinvo Web `Extrato` (portfolio statement),
  downloaded 66 seconds apart** — not two copies of the same report. `3_25` carries **42 `Aplicação`
  (buy) rows, all `Instituição = RICO`**; `3_26` carries **11 `Resgate` (sell) rows, all
  `Instituição = XP Investimentos`**. Together they cover 06–11/08/2026 and every row is `Tipo = Ação`.
- They are **movement statements, not positions**. No held quantity as of a date, no average price /
  cost basis, no current value, no proventos rows, no asset-class columns beyond the per-movement
  `Tipo`, no ISIN, no account, no row id. The only cost-like field is `Custo`, present only on
  `Resgate` rows (`4.90` × 9, `8.60` × 2), semantics not determinable from the files.
- They are a **downstream Kinvo view of the exact same trades** as the B3 Negociação sample
  (`references/movimentacoes_B3.xlsx`): `3_25` matches B3's `Compra` executions for ações/units
  execution-for-execution, `3_26` matches B3's `Venda` executions (with the 06/08 multi-fill sales
  aggregated to a 2-dp average price). FII and BDR rows are absent from both. **Nothing here is data
  B3 does not already carry.**
- **The closed #96 conclusion stands, with one factual refinement**: an XLSX export of the *Extrato*
  exists (empirically, these two files), but it exports **movements**, not positions — so "no
  positions export, no average price" is unchanged, and the recommended seed path (B3 Relatório
  Consolidado snapshot + B3 movements replay + manual RF/caixa) does not change.
- **No re-scope for "Kinvo as an ongoing source of balances"** — a balance is a position, and the
  extrato carries none. The only new candidate is "Kinvo extrato as an additional *movements*
  source", which is mostly redundant with B3 and only worth a follow-up if its two unverified
  properties pan out: `Custo` actually carrying operation costs (the fees gap in B3) or coverage of
  classes B3 does not export.

## 2. What the fields mean

- **This is the movement ledger of a Kinvo product, not a snapshot.** `Produto` + `Data` +
  `Descrição` (`Aplicação`/`Resgate`) + `Quantidade` + `Valor` describe a single operation, exactly
  the `Produto → Movimentações` model the Kinvo help center documents for registering
  applications/redemptions
  (<https://suporte.kinvo.com.br/articles/f5468f4f-6e51-4ce5-91d6-2c432ffe8f94>,
  <https://suporte.kinvo.com.br/articles/5c50738e-16a5-46ec-ada4-47e6287d3f24>, fetched 2026-09-16).
- **`Valor` is the unit price**, proven by the invariant `Valor Total = Quantidade × Valor` in all
  53 rows; `Valor Total` is the gross operation amount (no fees folded in; no fees visible anywhere
  in the buys file at all).
- **`Tipo` is per-movement and narrow** (`Ação` everywhere in the samples) — there is **no asset-class
  breakdown** (no FII/fundo/RF rows, no class column), and nothing separates ON/PN/UNT/DRN beyond the
  spec code embedded in the `Produto` name.
- **No positions fields**: no quantity-held, no average price, no cost basis, no closing/current
  price, no market value, no "as of" date. Nothing in these files can seed a position or a cost.
- **No proventos**: the only `Descrição` values are `Aplicação` and `Resgate`; no dividendo/JCP/rendimento
  rows. (Whether the Extrato *screen* has other movement types is not evidenced by these samples.)
- **`Instituição` is the only institution reference** (no CNPJ, no account number), and `Conexão` is
  empty in every row — so the files do not reveal which Kinvo connection/import the rows came from.
- **`Custo` is the one unexplained field.** It appears only on Resgates, as a constant per day
  (`4.90` on 10/08 and 11/08 rows, `8.60` on 06/08 rows) — on 06/08 both rows share `8.60` despite
  totals that differ almost 4×, so it is not a per-row percentage of anything. There is no
  counterpart column in the B3 export. Most plausibly a per-operation broker cost
  (corretagem/emolumentos) as recorded by Kinvo, but **this is an unverified inference** — do not
  rely on it without an in-account check of a broker nota.

## 3. Cross-check against the B3 sample (same trading window)

`references/movimentacoes_B3.xlsx` (parsed in `docs/research/2026-09-15-b3-extract.md`) covers the
same 06–11/08/2026 window: 82 executions at `XP INVESTIMENTOS CCTVM S/A.` — 59 `Compra`, 23 `Venda`.
Joining the Kinvo files against it (ticker matched with/without the fractional `F` suffix,
key = date + ticker + quantity + price):

- **`3_25` = exactly the B3 `Compra` rows for ações/units.** All 42 rows match a B3 `Compra` row to
  the cent. The 17 B3 `Compra` rows *not* in the file are exactly the 16 FII executions (`BRCO11`,
  `BTLG11`, `CPTS11`, `HGBS11` ×2, `HSML11`, `KNCR11`, `KNRI11`, `LVBI11`, `PCIP11`, `PVBI11`,
  `RBRR11`, `RBRX11`, `XPCI11`, `XPLG11`, `XPML11`) and the BDR `ROXO34` — consistent with a
  `Tipo = Ação` filter (inference).
- **`3_26` = the B3 `Venda` rows for ações/units, with two aggregated lines.** 9 of 11 rows match B3
  executions exactly; the other two are multi-fill aggregations on 06/08:
  - `EGIE3` 127 @ `29.90` in Kinvo = B3 executions 100@29.89 + 12@29.91 + 13@29.94 + 2@29.93.
    Kinvo's printed total is **3,797.30**, while the B3 executions sum to **3,797.00** (+0.30).
  - `EZTC3` 91 @ `11.03` = B3 executions 65@11.03 + 20@11.04 + 6@11.05. Kinvo total **1,003.73**
    vs B3 sum **1,004.05** (−0.32).
  In both cases the printed total equals `Quantidade × printed Valor` (the rounded 2-dp average
  price), not the exact execution sum — inference: Kinvo aggregates the fills and re-prices the
  aggregate at the rounded average. The 7 B3 `Venda` rows absent from `3_26` are again exactly the
  FII sales (`HGRU11` ×2, `KNSC11`, `MCCI11`, `MXRF11`, `RBRP11`, `VILG11`).
- **One unexplained discrepancy:** B3 attributes every execution to `XP INVESTIMENTOS CCTVM S/A.`,
  while Kinvo's files label all buys `RICO` and all sells `XP Investimentos`. Whether Kinvo's label
  reflects its own connection/product naming rather than the executing broker is not determinable
  from these files (out of scope; flagged for the import design).

Implication: for the listed trades these exports are **redundant with the B3 Negociação export** —
same data, one less execution granularity, plus a cost column of unknown meaning.

## 4. Which screen/report produced them; documented or ad-hoc?

- Evidence says: **the `Extrato` screen of the Kinvo portfolio (Carteira → Extrato), exported via a
  download action, with filters applied** (inference from: worksheet and filename stem both
  `Extrato`/`extrato-carteira`; columns matching the movement model including the
  connection-specific `Instituição`/`Conexão` pair; server-side generation timestamps; two files =
  two filterings 66 s apart). The plans comparison lists `Extrato` as a product feature
  (<https://consolidador.kinvo.com.br/planos/>, fetched 2026-09-16).
- **The export itself is not publicly documented.** The #96 scan of the help center
  (`suporte.kinvo.com.br` and the legacy Freshdesk) found no article about exporting positions, and
  this pass over the current knowledge base adds none; the articles describe the movement
  registration/editing UI, not an export
  (<https://suporte.kinvo.com.br/solutions>, fetched 2026-09-16). So: a **real product export
  (evidenced by these files), repeatable on demand, but undocumented** — unlike the IR auxiliary
  XLSX, which is documented.
- Because the files are machine-generated XLSX with correct data (not an HTML scrape), they are not
  an ad-hoc screen scrape — but the exact UI affordance (button/menu), its filter set, and whether
  it is plan-gated were not verifiable without an authenticated session. The two files show it can
  at least be filtered down to one `Descrição` + institution at a time.

## 5. Verdict

1. **Does it change the closed #96 conclusion?** Only by refinement. #96 said "no general-purpose
   export of current positions; the only Excel output is the Premium, web-only IR auxiliary report".
   The evidence adds: an XLSX export of the **Extrato (movements)** is produced by the product, so
   the IR report is not the only Excel output that exists *in practice* — but the extrato carries
   **no positions and no average price**, so the operational conclusion stands: *Kinvo cannot seed
   positions or cost basis; it remains reference-only for the seed*.
2. **Does it change the recommended seed path?** No. The path stays **B3 Relatório Consolidado
   (position snapshot, quantity only) + B3 movements replay + manual RF/caixa and cost entry**,
   exactly as in `docs/research/2026-09-15-b3-extract.md`. The extrato's `Custo` is not an
   acquisition price and appears only on sells; the extrato in these samples covers only ações.
3. **Does "Kinvo as an ongoing source of balances" deserve a re-scope on the map?** **No.** Balances
   are positions, and this export has none. The only map-worthy nuance is a new *candidate*: "Kinvo
   Extrato XLSX as an additional movements source" — which is redundant with B3 for listed assets,
   so it should stay closed unless a follow-up establishes one of its two unverified edges: `Custo`
   carrying real operation costs (the fees gap that B3 leaves open) or coverage of classes B3 does
   not export (RF/Tesouro/proventos — none present in these samples).

## 6. Open uncertainties

- Meaning/formula of `Custo` (only on Resgates; `4.90`/`8.60`; constant per day; no counterpart in B3).
- Whether an **unfiltered** extrato export exists and what it contains: FIIs, renda fixa, Tesouro,
  proventos, transfers, câmbio rows, other `Tipo`s. The two samples are ações-only filtered views.
- Which plan tier the export action is available on (the `Extrato` feature is listed on the plans
  page; `Relatórios em PDF e Excel` is a Premium row, but that is a different feature).
- The exact UI affordance and filter set that produced these files (not verifiable unauthenticated),
  and whether the user or the map wants Kinvo as a second movement feed at all.
- The `RICO` vs `XP Investimentos` institution labelling on identical B3 executions.
- Kinvo's aggregation rule for multi-fill sales (only two examples; totals deviate ±0.30 from the
  execution sums because they use the rounded average price).
- Dedup implications if ever imported: no row id, no time, pre-aggregated rows — a natural-key dedup
  is weaker than B3's (which at least has execution rows); not designed here.
